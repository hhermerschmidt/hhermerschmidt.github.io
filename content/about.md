Senior software engineer with a strong focus on software architecture and the long-term evolution of large-scale C++ simulation systems, bridging numerical methods, physical modelling, and robust software design.

Currently at **MAGMA GmbH** (Aachen), where I work on the solver core of a commercial multi-physics casting simulation platform.

## What I work on

- **Software architecture** for large-scale scientific and HPC codebases (C++17/20)
- **Solver development**: FVM, FEM, CFD, free-surface flow, FSI, multi-physics coupling
- **API design and modularisation** — making solver components composable and reusable across physical regimes and solver generations
- **Legacy modernisation**: decomposing tightly coupled C/C++ systems into modular, testable, maintainable designs

## Background

PhD in Computational Engineering (TU Braunschweig, *summa cum laude*), with a focus on space-time finite element methods and fluid-structure interaction. Previously a scientific assistant and DFG project lead at TU Braunschweig's Institute of Structural Analysis.

## Interests

Interested in the intersection of classical simulation methods and machine learning — how physics-based modelling and data-driven approaches complement each other in engineering workflows.
